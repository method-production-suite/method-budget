# Method Budget 0.8.1

A small release that makes the budget easier to see with a high contrast theme, and shows
focus on an account with no lines yet.

## Fixed

- **High contrast themes.** With a contrast theme on in Windows, selected rows and ranges
  were hard to see and the cursor could disappear. Selection now takes the system's
  highlight color, the cursor keeps a solid ring, a cell with a problem gets a dashed ring,
  and the marks for adjustments and comments show as borders.
- **An empty account shows focus.** An account with no lines showed that it had focus only
  by a faint border. It now shows the same ring as everything else.

## Good to know

- **Everyone sharing a budget needs 0.8.0 or later.** Method Budget 0.7.0 and older refuse
  a shared budget once its owner's key is in.
- **Mac builds are universal.** Windows and Linux builds are included but not yet tested
  by hand. The Windows build is not signed, and no certificate is planned: Windows warns
  that the publisher is unknown before it opens.
