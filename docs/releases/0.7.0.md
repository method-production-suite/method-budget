# Method Budget 0.7.0

Incentive programs are complete, with what-ifs and a printed report by location. You can
keep your own rate tables and lay out your own bids, and the start window organizes
budgets into projects.

## Incentive programs, all the way

- **Minimum spends, broker's fees and shared caps.** A program can require a minimum
  qualified spend, take a flat broker's fee off after its cap and discount, and share a cap
  with other programs. Set them in Budget Setup's Credits tab.
- **What if these lines were shot somewhere else.** The Credits tab's What if… panel moves
  a category's, an account's or chosen lines to another location on a copy and shows every
  program's estimate before and after, the difference, and the net total. Apply it as one
  undo step, or leave it.
- **An incentives report by location.** A new report section lists each location's spend,
  the programs counting it and every program's terms, with totals that match the topsheet's
  credits. It prints, makes a PDF, and appears as an Incentives table in spreadsheet
  (.xlsx) and CSV exports of a budget with credits.
- **Estimates show their figures.** A program you haven't applied shows its row figures,
  marked as an estimate.

## Rate tables you keep

- **Your own tables of rates.** Budget Setup's Rate Tables tab keeps classifications with a
  rate per unit, dates in effect and notes. Import one from your own spreadsheet by
  mapping its columns.
- **Fill a line's rate from a table.** Fill Rate from Table… on a line writes the rate once
  and remembers where it came from; Explain says so. When the table changes, Update rate
  is offered, and no figure changes on its own.
- **No rates come with the app.** Every table is one you make or import.

## Bids laid out your way

- **A bid layout in a report preset.** Name your own header fields, make sections that take
  accounts by category, by account or by a range of account numbers, and add running
  subtotals and markups (a percent of chosen sections or a flat amount). The Bid section
  prints it in the preview and the PDF.
- **Every account counts once.** An account no section takes is printed under Unassigned,
  so the bid always adds up to the budget's net total plus its markups.
- **One plain example layout** comes with the app to start from.

## The start window, finished

- **Projects.** Folders down the left of the start window hold a project's budgets,
  sub-budgets, copies, setup packs and schedule files. Add Folder… adds one, and New
  Budget can make a project folder for the new budget.
- **Thumbnails.** Each budget shows a small bar for each category's total.
- **Duplicate, and a sub-budget from a row.** Duplicate saves a copy beside the file. New
  Sub-Budget from This Category or Account makes a smaller budget from a topsheet row,
  with the same totals.
- **Link a schedule when you start.** New Budget's Link a Schedule… fills the shoot days
  and the dates from a schedule's days file.
- **Sorting, and who was in.** Sort by last opened, name, last changed or net total.
  Shared budgets show who was in them lately.

## Fixed

- **Very long formulas.** A formula as long as the app allows, thousands of terms, is
  worked out without running short of memory on any computer.
- **Safer connections for updates.** The libraries that check secure connections are
  updated for recently published advisories.

## Good to know

- **The app is getting ready for other languages.** Most of its words now come from one
  table. English is still the only language.
- **Windows and Linux builds are included but not yet tested by hand.**
